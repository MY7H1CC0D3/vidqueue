VidQueue - queue videos from Firefox, max 3 downloads per site
================================================================

1. Double-click "Install VidQueue.bat" (no admin rights needed).
   Choose your options:
     - Start with Windows: VidQueue keeps running in the background
       (this always shows the tray icon so you can see and quit it)
     - Show tray icon: optional otherwise
     - Install the Firefox extension: Firefox opens and asks to add it -
       click Add
   Then click Install.

2. That's it. Firefox starts VidQueue automatically when it's needed, the
   same way Video DownloadHelper's companion app works. Without "Start with
   Windows", VidQueue closes itself after Firefox exits, once any videos it
   was finishing are saved.

3. Open a video page. The VidQueue button shows a number when it finds a
   video. Click it and choose "Queue best video". You can also right-click a
   link > "VidQueue: queue video from this link". If the button isn't on the
   toolbar, click the puzzle-piece icon > gear next to VidQueue > Pin to
   Toolbar.

Notes
- Firefox must stay open while videos download (it does the downloading,
  which is what gets past sites protected by Cloudflare). Downloads that
  were interrupted continue the next time Firefox runs.
- Downloads go to Downloads\VidQueue until you pick a folder in the
  extension (popup > Default... or Settings). "This session..." picks a
  folder only until Firefox is closed.
- The dashboard (extension popup > Dashboard, or Start menu > VidQueue)
  shows your queue. Its About window has the Start with Windows and tray
  icon settings and a Quit button.
- The extension updates itself; new versions of the app show a banner in
  the dashboard.
- Everything stays on your PC. Settings and the queue are kept in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\VidQueue.
- Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > VidQueue. Remove the
  extension in Firefox (Add-ons and themes).

Windows may warn that the setup or app is from an unknown publisher (it
isn't code-signed). Click "More info" > "Run anyway".
